What you can do

Every pitch you need

Full, three-quarter, middle third, half pitch and a plain training area, horizontal or vertical, with optional grids and goals.

160+ real kits

Pick club or national team colours, or set your own fill, border and jersey style. Formation presets from 4-3-3 to 5-3-2 lay the team out in one click.

Animate the move

Add a step, move the players, add another. Táctica tweens between them and draws the movement trails, then exports an animated GIF or MP4.

Annotate clearly

Run, pass and line arrows with adjustable curve, zones, spotlights, vision cones, player links, callouts and headlines.

Export and share

PNG or JPG at presentation quality, animated GIF, MP4 video, or a public link anyone can open without an account.

Your club badge

Drop a team crest onto the pitch as a faint watermark and lock it in place.

Questions coaches ask

Is the tactics board free?

Yes. The tactical board, the animation steps and image export are free to use, and you can start without creating an account. Signing in saves your work to the cloud so you can open it on another device.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Táctica runs entirely in the browser on Mac, Windows, iPad and phones. There is no app to install and no plugin.

Can I animate player movement?

Yes. The Step tool records the position of every player and object as a keyframe. Move things, add another step, and Táctica animates between them. You can export the result as a GIF or video.

Can I share a board with my players?

Yes. Export it as an image, or create a public link that opens in any browser without an account.
