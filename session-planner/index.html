What you can do

Draw the drill

Players, cones, disc cones, ladders, poles, hoops, mannequins and small goals on a zoomed-in training area — not a full pitch the exercise gets lost on.

A library that scales

Filter by category, sort by last edited or duration, and search names, descriptions and objectives. Every drill card shows its board, length, player count and kit list.

Equipment list for free

Táctica counts the gear on the pitch as you draw, so each drill and each session carries its own setup checklist.

Build the session

Order drills warm-up to cool-down, set objectives and player counts, and see total session time update as you go.

Print it properly

A clean PDF with the board picture beside each drill, its description, coaching points, progressions and the equipment you need.

Duplicate and vary

Copy a drill in one click to build a progression without redrawing it.

Questions coaches ask

Can I print a training session?

Yes. Print / PDF produces a one-page-per-few-drills sheet with the board picture next to each drill, plus its description, coaching points, progressions and equipment list.

Are my drills saved?

Signed in, drills and sessions save to your account and are available on any device. Signed out, they save in that browser only.

What equipment can I put on the pitch?

Balls, cones, disc cones, small goals, agility ladders, slalom poles, hoops and free-kick mannequins, alongside players, zones, arrows and text.

Can I reuse a drill in several sessions?

Yes. Drills live in your library independently, so the same drill can appear in as many sessions as you like, and duplicating one lets you build variations.
